Privacy notice

Lumman Energy uses the personal information you submit through this website to respond to enquiries, assess quotation requests and provide requested services. Information may include your name, contact details, Eircode, property information and files you choose to upload.

Quote form submissions and uploads are processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form service, before being delivered to Lumman Energy by email. Please do not submit information that is not needed for your quotation.

We do not sell your personal information. We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, quotations, legal obligations and business records.

To ask about, correct or request deletion of personal information you have submitted, contact lummanenergy@outlook.com.
