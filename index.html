About Lumman Energy

Created to help Irish households take more control of energy costs.

Lumman Energy was founded after seeing the benefits solar can bring to Irish households facing high electricity costs. Our aim is straightforward: help homeowners and businesses produce more of their own energy with clear advice, professional solutions and dependable aftercare.

We work across Sligo, Leitrim, Mayo, Galway and Roscommon, providing solar PV, battery storage, EV charging and yearly maintenance contracts.
