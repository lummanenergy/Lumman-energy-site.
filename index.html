About Lumman Energy

Built around the benefits solar can bring to Irish households.

Lumman Energy was started after seeing first-hand the benefits solar can bring to Irish households facing high energy costs. Our goal is simple: help homeowners produce more of their own electricity and make the move to renewable energy straightforward.

We work with customers across Connacht on solar PV, battery storage and EV charging projects, with free quotations and yearly maintenance contracts available.
